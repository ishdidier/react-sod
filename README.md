INTEGRATED SITUATION
Cafe Camellia is a high-end coffee shop that specializes in breakfast, coffee, tea, pastries,
juices, fruits, and other delicious foods. It is currently located at M-Peace Plaza (Makuza
Building, Kigali, Rwanda), Car Free Zone area, Chic Building, KCM Building (former UTC), MIC
Building, KBC Building, and Camellia Kisimenti. They also have a wing called Camellia Catering
Services.
They recruit employees by administering written interview exams and selecting the best
candidate(s) for different positions. Currently, they use a file-based system (paper
documents) to store and manage candidates' results. This has led to several issues, including
poor security, limited accessibility, lack of data integrity, and inefficiency in reporting
interview results.
Your Task:
Design and develop a web application for managing Café Camellia's recruitment process.
Use the following technologies:
 Preferred Stack:
o Frontend: React
o Backend: Node.js with Express
